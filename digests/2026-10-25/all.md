# Hacker tied to Ticketmaster's Snowflake breach pleads guilty, as a Carnegie Mellon researcher questions data agents

The clearest news of the day is legal: a guilty plea in the Snowflake customer-account breach campaign that hit Ticketmaster and over a hundred other companies. Analytics engineering produced the day's best ideas piece — a grounded case for why data agents still can't do real analysis — plus practical guidance on migrating to dbt v2, while data engineering's strongest signal came from two practitioner write-ups on Iceberg late data and the Spark-vs-DuckDB/Polars cost tradeoff.
