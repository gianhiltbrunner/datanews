# Provider-agnostic lakehouse contracts headline a wire-noise-heavy day

The Databricks/Snowflake-tagged Google News searches returned almost entirely stock analysis, local crime and sports stories with no data-platform relevance today, and the ClickHouse release feed carried only routine LTS and stable point releases.
