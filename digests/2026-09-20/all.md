# dbt Summit's agent-ready pitch and AI "context layers" dominate a thin news day

Original reporting was scarce today: data-engineering and data-platform feeds were mostly SEO content and off-topic wire noise, with no releases from the usual orchestration, engine or catalog projects. The clearest throughline was AI-agent tooling — LinkedIn's MCP-based context layer for coding agents, and a parallel push in analytics engineering to give AI agents context on dbt models — while trade press kept recapping dbt Labs' and Fivetran's dbt v2, dbt State and "agent-ready" pitch from dbt Summit 2026.
