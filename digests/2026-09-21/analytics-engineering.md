# dbt Labs' summit puts dbt v2 and the Fivetran merger center stage

dbt Labs used its annual summit to showcase dbt v2 and outline joint channel plans with its new merger partner Fivetran, and a practitioner migration write-up the same day catalogs the breakage that comes with moving onto dbt Core's Rust engine. Elsewhere, dbt-core shipped a patch for a BigQuery incremental-model bug, and Lightdash and Cube both pushed updates, one adding semantic-chart and MCP features, the other patching a dependency CVE.

## Releases & tools
- **[v2.0.5](https://github.com/dbt-labs/dbt/releases/tag/v2.0.5)** — dbt-core. Fixes a bug where the BigQuery driver could silently use `REPLACE TABLE` instead of `MERGE` for incremental tables after an ADBC `GetObjects` 404/403 response.
- **[2.272.2](https://github.com/lightdash/lightdash/releases/tag/2.272.2)** — Lightdash. The newest patch in today's 2.272.x run, which also let users explore semantic charts sourced from documents, added MCP-guided document research summaries, and exposed AI agent names in usage analytics.
- **[v1.7.42](https://github.com/cube-js/cube/releases/tag/v1.7.42)** — Cube. Follows v1.7.41, which replaced the unmaintained, CVE-flagged `extract-zip` dependency with `node-stream-zip` and cleared 52 Dependabot alerts.
