# Oracle adds a business semantic layer to its Autonomous AI Lakehouse

Oracle's push to ground AI agents in business context with a new semantic layer dominated the wire today, but the more useful reading came from two practitioner write-ups on keeping incremental dbt and warehouse pipelines correct under reruns and reprocessing. Lightdash and SQLGlot both shipped notable releases, the latter with an explicit breaking change in its optimizer.

## Top stories
- **[Oracle adds a semantic layer to ground AI agents in the business context](https://news.google.com/rss/articles/CBMiuwFBVV95cUxNdm5jWlJtQzVvTzdkQUptYURsVjlfbi1tMW9DTU9SeXFIUDN1QVRuN2RZZ1BWREZ4LWEydUVNN0RfU1Y0OGNXUmZLWXZxbzh5S3RyTjhZSTVHT1EzS1RPXzFqcDg5N19VNTVubzY3VGRIZlVYRXJybTdreGM0U2RuLUNhRVlsU3pKYWVSUTVzM0xkbkJWbFRQVUtnZVd2cEJZSnhTMGIwRXNhUTRsYUZzLU9SR1RvcXVEU01J?oc=5)** — InfoWorld (Google News). Oracle announced a business semantic layer, an AI planning engine and expanded Iceberg support for its Autonomous AI Lakehouse, aimed at stopping AI agents from misreading business data. *Why it matters:* another major vendor is betting that semantic layers, not bigger models, are what makes AI agents trustworthy on business questions.
- **[Reprocessing BigQuery at Scale: The Hidden Failure Modes of Incremental dbt Pipelines](https://medium.com/dbt-for-analytics/reprocessing-bigquery-at-scale-the-hidden-failure-modes-of-incremental-dbt-pipelines-b913cf3fd847?source=rss------dbt-5)** — dbt for Analytics (Medium). Covers designing deterministic backfills, safe partition replacement and dependency-aware recovery for incremental dbt models on BigQuery. *Why it matters:* incremental models are where most dbt pipelines quietly break, and this gets specific about the failure modes.
- **[A bank-filings warehouse built for reruns, duplicates and renames](https://medium.com/@smhassanraza1/a-bank-filings-warehouse-built-for-reruns-duplicates-and-renames-150fa7502547?source=rss------dbt-5)** — Medium. Walks through turning quarterly FDIC filings into tested BigQuery tables and the four failure cases — reruns, duplicates, renames and more — that shaped the pipeline's design. *Why it matters:* a concrete case study in designing for idempotency against a messy real-world source.
- **[Does Better Data Modelling Actually Improve AI Analytics?](https://medium.com/@achmad.rozie/does-better-data-modelling-actually-improve-ai-analytics-7e3894d1e244?source=rss------dbt-5)** — Medium. Runs 900 attempts across six data-access configurations and two AI models to compare correctness, consistency and cost of letting an LLM answer business questions. *Why it matters:* rare empirical evidence, rather than vendor claims, on whether modelling effort actually helps AI-driven analytics.
