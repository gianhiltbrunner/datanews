# DuckDB 2.0 LTS ships agent mode and a server protocol

A quiet day for pure pipeline news, but DuckDB's first LTS release is a genuinely big deal, and Netflix published a detailed look at how it diagnoses failures across 100,000 daily workloads. Elsewhere, a sharp opinion piece pushed back on "streamhouse" hype, and two practitioner write-ups covered the real trade-offs of Snowflake Openflow CDC and silently corrupted ERP extracts.

## Top stories
- **[DuckDB 2.0.0 LTS "Cinnamon Teal" released](https://duckdb.org/2026/10/21/announcing-duckdb-200)** — Robin Moffatt (Bluesky). DuckDB's first long-term-support release adds an "agent mode," a server mode with its own "quack protocol," and triggers. *Why it matters:* it's the biggest DuckDB release yet, and the LTS label signals DuckDB Labs is positioning it for production stability.
- **[Making Failure Diagnosis Reliable with Pensive AI](https://netflixtechblog.com/making-failure-diagnosis-reliable-with-pensive-ai-6b0811a1c015?source=rss----2615bd06b42e---4)** — Netflix Tech Blog. Netflix describes Pensive AI, built to triage the roughly 100,000 workload failures a day across Titus, Spark, Trino and Druid and tell engineers whether the cause is their code or the platform. *Why it matters:* a concrete account of automating failure triage at a scale few teams operate at.
- **[Streamhouse Is the New Buzzword. Here's the Part That Actually Matters.](https://dev.to/andrew_tan_layline/streamhouse-is-the-new-buzzword-heres-the-part-that-actually-matters-1c7e)** — DEV. Argues that vendors pitching "streamhouse" as the successor to the lakehouse are overselling the term, and that it doesn't address the real problem most teams are stuck on. *Why it matters:* a contrarian read on a term Confluent, Aiven and Redpanda are all pushing hard right now.
- **[Snowflake Openflow Limitations: 6 Production Trade-offs](https://dev.to/estuary/snowflake-openflow-limitations-6-production-trade-offs-4322)** — DEV (Estuary). Catalogs six recurring trade-offs teams hit running database CDC on Snowflake's NiFi-based Openflow, from fixed runtime sizing to an unfinished Gen 1 to Gen 2 transition. *Why it matters:* a practical checklist for anyone evaluating Openflow for CDC beyond the marketing pitch.
- **[Your ERP extract is 31% wrong. Nothing errored.](https://dev.to/trcollinson/your-erp-extract-is-31-wrong-nothing-errored-2hf8)** — DEV. Walks through why a naive ERP extractor silently got 30.8% of rows wrong, the six defenses that fixed it, and a near-$0 serverless AWS pattern (EventBridge, Step Functions, Lambda, Iceberg on Glue) for doing it right. *Why it matters:* silent data corruption from "working" extracts is a common and underreported failure mode.
