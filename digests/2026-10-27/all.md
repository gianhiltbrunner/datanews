# DuckDB 2.0 LTS release headlines a day of major data tooling news

The day's biggest story is DuckDB's first long-term-support release, but the real substance came from practitioner write-ups: Netflix on automated failure diagnosis at scale, and AWS customers the New York Times and Vestiaire Collective on quantified migration and upgrade wins. Oracle also made a significant push into AI-ready semantic layers for its Autonomous AI Lakehouse.

## Top 5 across domains
- **[Data Engineering]** **[DuckDB 2.0.0 LTS "Cinnamon Teal" released](https://duckdb.org/2026/10/21/announcing-duckdb-200)** — DuckDB's biggest release yet adds an agent mode, a server mode with its own protocol, and triggers, under a new LTS label.
- **[Data Platform Engineering]** **[The New York Times cut Oracle migration time from 18 to 5 months](https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/database/the-new-york-times-cut-oracle-migration-time-from-18-to-5-months/)** — an AI-agent-native migration from Oracle to Aurora PostgreSQL, automated with Bedrock and AWS DMS.
- **[Analytics Engineering]** **[Oracle adds a semantic layer to ground AI agents in the business context](https://news.google.com/rss/articles/CBMiuwFBVV95cUxNdm5jWlJtQzVvTzdkQUptYURsVjlfbi1tMW9DTU9SeXFIUDN1QVRuN2RZZ1BWREZ4LWEydUVNN0RfU1Y0OGNXUmZLWXZxbzh5S3RyTjhZSTVHT1EzS1RPXzFqcDg5N19VNTVubzY3VGRIZlVYRXJybTdreGM0U2RuLUNhRVlsU3pKYWVSUTVzM0xkbkJWbFRQVUtnZVd2cEJZSnhTMGIwRXNhUTRsYUZzLU9SR1RvcXVEU01J?oc=5)** — Oracle's new semantic layer and AI planning engine for its Autonomous AI Lakehouse, aimed at keeping agents from misreading business data.
- **[Data Engineering]** **[Making Failure Diagnosis Reliable with Pensive AI](https://netflixtechblog.com/making-failure-diagnosis-reliable-with-pensive-ai-6b0811a1c015?source=rss----2615bd06b42e---4)** — how Netflix automates triage of roughly 100,000 daily workload failures across Titus, Spark, Trino and Druid.
- **[Data Platform Engineering]** **[How Vestiaire Collective achieved near-zero downtime RDS for MariaDB upgrades](https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/database/how-vestiaire-collective-achieved-near-zero-downtime-rds-for-mariadb-upgrades-with-amazon-rds-blue-green-deployments/)** — RDS Blue/Green deployments took a major-version upgrade switchover down to 7 seconds during peak season.
