# Metrics standards fragment across ODCS, ODPS, Ossie and dbt MetricFlow

No single release or announcement defined the day; the feed was dominated by near-identical vendor posts pitching semantic layers for AI agents (Denodo, AtScale, Dell, Neo4j all ran variations on the same pitch). The more useful reading came from independent writers working through dbt internals and warehouse design choices.

## Top stories
- **[One Metric, Four Standards](https://medium.com/@alex.nykolaiszyn/one-metric-four-standards-77fc3c5e2007?source=rss------dbt-5)** — Medium. Argues that ODCS, ODPS, Ossie and dbt's MetricFlow each solve only part of defining a metric consistently across a data platform, and that no standard yet covers the whole problem. *Why it matters:* teams building semantic layers today are choosing between incomplete standards, not a settled one.
