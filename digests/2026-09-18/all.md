# dbt v2.0 ships as GA while Snowflake goes sovereign and Databricks' IPO math gets scrutinized

dbt Labs shipped the biggest architectural change to dbt in years — a Rust-rewritten engine, now GA under the plain name "dbt" — while Snowflake struck an EU sovereign-cloud deal with STACKIT and analysts publicly questioned Databricks' $190 billion pre-IPO valuation even as it keeps expanding in Asia. Data engineering feeds were quieter, but Intuit's account of AWS fault-injection testing and a DuckDB-tool testing war story were the standouts.

## Top 5 across domains
- **[Analytics Engineering]** **[dbt v2.0 is GA](https://docs.getdbt.com/blog/dbt-v2-is-ga)** — the Fusion engine, rewritten in Rust, is now the default "dbt" distribution: 4x faster compiles, local SQL comprehension that catches errors before they hit the warehouse, and a 10x-smaller Parquet-based metadata format, alongside new Fivetran integration announcements at dbt Summit.
- **[Data Platform Engineering]** **[Snowflake Integrates with STACKIT for EU Data Sovereignty](https://www.snowflake.com/content/snowflake-site/global/en/blog/snowflake-integration-stackit-data-sovereignty)** — Snowflake's tie-up with Germany's sovereign STACKIT cloud keeps encryption keys under European control and data in open Iceberg tables, addressing regulated industries' concerns about foreign legal reach.
- **[Data Platform Engineering]** **[Databricks Piles Investments Into Asia](https://news.google.com/rss/articles/CBMinwFBVV95cUxNYkIzd0RoTVgyNlV5TzhGb1ZWZy15ZkhGZnJoYVFLZzhYRzdyX0lZcjAzRlJkSWdlMGVtM3daQ0FTV3BHWWwwVDZlUTZSYUVBbFVjV1ZGVUZ0empkLVp5Wk91UFZaazRKZ1pBVndzZU5tc3AwS05JNS15UGZsNFRacmdzcE5WVHo2ZFpxVVNnRy03NFZHbllLZ0RtVi1fZW8?oc=5)** as growth accelerates, even as a Morningstar analysis argues the math behind its **[$190 billion valuation doesn't add up](https://news.google.com/rss/articles/CBMiuwFBVV95cUxPWU9DN21XWjd1b24zNENjc1hjMU8wUW02VHhubWF2N0hhMk44MTIwM2xZSzV4eXExbWVOQnV4QVR3cmpTcW5Vd1M0ZFJpckM3UExKUjY1VjI2Q1NVODNvc09ZMWRJTUpEa2pVOW9OekdMc0Rfckc3NEl1Zl9XeEhUVEtjZC1NRFBRRW12dW1LRkFvQ2hQMzJDS0lPTldRc1RWQmdzOXkwWjEwY05xNU05YkUyTTNUaVo1RHcw?oc=5)** ahead of a widely expected IPO.
