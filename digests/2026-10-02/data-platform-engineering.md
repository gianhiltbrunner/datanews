# Databricks Raises $5 Billion at a $190 Billion Valuation

Databricks closed a $5 billion funding round valuing the company at $190 billion, while Microsoft and Google threw their weight behind Apache Ossie, a new project aimed at making enterprise data and AI platforms interoperable. LinkedIn and PayPal each published detailed accounts of scaling their data platforms, and Databricks separately showed off a Lakebase feature that restores 100 TB Postgres databases in seconds.

## Top stories
- **[SBVA Joins Databricks' $5 Billion Funding Round at $190 Billion Valuation](https://news.google.com/rss/articles/CBMidkFVX3lxTFA4VUkyTm1feXFHdGVVQl9oSEU2TEFkZFh5LU1OOVhpbks1bnZzYU5oZDhsNXVJeC10N3BRYlJFeW5SRVRyYTZCd2lETFpEMkpQUE5mU1JWaHZwT1VNd0JBdDlVeEJ2dnI0b0lMOS1zYk9xWHRoUXc?oc=5)** — Google News. Databricks closed a $5 billion funding round that values the company at $190 billion, with South Korea's SBVA among the investors. *Why it matters:* cements Databricks as one of the most highly valued private data companies, intensifying competition with Snowflake and the cloud vendors.
- **[Microsoft, Google back Apache Ossie to make enterprise data and AI platforms more interoperable](https://news.google.com/rss/articles/CBMi2wFBVV95cUxNNjhwM1VpSEdOXzZjX1hCZHg2OXBrY1U2Y1l1ZHR4R0wzQVBnbXYta044WFVOdVlQRU5QcVpNMXRDODNRaTNMeG5GWUlRdy1EREFyZWFZemc2eXhBd2puc3dGeV9tM0xnLTVzSW5uejFzTjBmcWVfODVZTWpDemFrMmxvLTBDQVhnZ19xSXRZWjRCNURDQ2xOSlAtekNkMUwwNTU3REl4c1NOWFpNWnZMWXlnbFNZX0lmRjJmcVhRRzdqRC1BMWpxOWZXRHF0Q3pOUmZBVmdjVV9MNkU?oc=5)** — InfoWorld, via Google News. Microsoft and Google are backing Apache Ossie, a new project for interoperable enterprise data and AI platforms, with early coverage also flagging Microsoft-ecosystem support and a Power BI converter. *Why it matters:* cross-vendor backing for a shared interoperability layer could ease the integration tax data platform teams pay across clouds.
- **[How LinkedIn extended ClickHouse from distributed tracing to metric discovery and analytics](https://clickhouse.com/blog/linkedin-observability-at-scale)** — ClickHouse Blog. LinkedIn extended its ClickHouse-based observability stack from distributed tracing into metric discovery and analytics, consolidating more than 13 billion metrics into one index serving over 150,000 queries per minute. *Why it matters:* a concrete, large-scale reference architecture for teams considering ClickHouse for observability beyond tracing.
- **[Lakebase Postgres branch-based restores for fast recovery at scale](https://www.databricks.com/blog/lakebase-postgres-branch-based-restores-fast-recovery-scale)** — Databricks Blog. Lakebase's branch-based restore creates a metadata pointer to a historical point in its decoupled storage instead of replaying transaction logs, so restoring a 100 TB Postgres database takes seconds rather than hours. *Why it matters:* point-in-time recovery time has long scaled with database size; this breaks that link for Postgres-based OLTP.
- **[Accelerating analytics: PayPal's journey with Managed Service for Apache Spark](https://cloud.google.com/blog/products/data-analytics/paypals-journey-with-managed-service-for-apache-spark/)** — Google Cloud — Data Analytics. PayPal describes moving off a complex, self-assembled on-prem analytics stack onto Google Cloud's Managed Service for Apache Spark to keep up with scaling and maintenance demands. *Why it matters:* another large enterprise's account of migrating off home-grown Spark infrastructure onto a managed service.
