# Dell joins the race to give AI agents a semantic layer of business context

Dell's launch of AI data-platform tools aimed at giving agents business context drew the most industry attention today, a sign that the semantic/context layer is becoming contested ground beyond the usual dbt/Cube/Snowflake players. On the engineering side, Netflix published a cluster of posts including word that its discontinued open-source orchestrator Conductor has more to its story, Ridgeline showed a concrete win from pairing Iceberg with ClickHouse Cloud, and Snowflake engineers flagged that nobody yet has a clean way to say what an AI agent's query actually cost.

## Top 5 across domains
- **[Data Platform Engineering]** [How Ridgeline unified Iceberg and ClickHouse into one analytics layer for investment management](https://clickhouse.com/blog/ridgeline-iceberg-clickhouse-cloud) — a real customer scaled to 10x its previous capacity by querying Iceberg history directly from ClickHouse Cloud instead of copying it in.
- **[Analytics Engineering]** [How Dell hopes to claim the context layer](https://news.google.com/rss/articles/CBMiaEFVX3lxTE5tY2dHeUhHYUdDS1otVFdCVnpodXY3eG1sbndlcjVDZlVueW8tS1NDYnZ3SHphZ2xiREQzR3JBLU11SGpyN2xKVWVlWnE1ekFhZTZjYW1pU0pBTVlZSms3RW1NdkhTYjZt?oc=5) — Dell's new AI data-platform tools push into territory long held by semantic-layer vendors.
- **[Data Engineering]** [Netflix Conductor: The Next Chapter](https://netflixtechblog.com/netflix-conductor-the-next-chapter-41ad21067649?source=rss----2615bd06b42e---4) — the orchestration engine Netflix ended as open source in 2023 turns out not to be finished internally.
- **[Data Platform Engineering]** [From Prompt to Query: Real Cost of Agents on Snowflake](https://medium.com/snowflake/from-prompt-to-query-real-cost-of-agents-on-snowflake-f4380037d553?source=rss----34b6daafc07---4) — unlike a dashboard's fixed query, each agent interaction takes a different path through Cortex inference, search and warehouses, and nobody has a single view of what any one of them cost.
- **[Data Engineering]** [DataJunction as Netflix's answer to the missing piece of the modern data stack](https://netflixtechblog.com/datajunction-as-netflixs-answer-to-the-missing-piece-of-the-modern-data-stack-92af926b40a5?source=rss----2615bd06b42e---4) — an open-source metrics platform built to give Netflix's dashboards and models one consistent number instead of duplicated metric logic.
