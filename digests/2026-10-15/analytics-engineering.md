# Dell wades into the semantic layer with a push to give AI agents business context

Dell's launch of AI data-platform tools built to hand business context to AI agents dominated the news feed, prompting analyst commentary on whether it can compete with the incumbent semantic-layer vendors. On the practitioner side, dbt Labs detailed the agent that powers its new "Wizard" feature, and two Medium write-ups covered real pipeline breakage and AI-generated dbt code.

## Top stories
- **[How Dell hopes to claim the context layer](https://news.google.com/rss/articles/CBMiaEFVX3lxTE5tY2dHeUhHYUdDS1otVFdCVnpodXY3eG1sbndlcjVDZlVueW8tS1NDYnZ3SHphZ2xiREQzR3JBLU11SGpyN2xKVWVlWnE1ekFhZTZjYW1pU0pBTVlZSms3RW1NdkhTYjZt?oc=5)** — Diginomica. Dell announced AI data-platform tools meant to give AI agents business context and connect information across systems, part of a wider push analysts are framing as a land grab for the "context layer" that semantic-layer vendors have long occupied. *Why it matters:* another large vendor entering semantic-layer territory raises the stakes for incumbents like dbt Labs' Semantic Layer and Cube.
- **[Inside the dbt Wizard harness: building an agent that thinks in DAGs](https://docs.getdbt.com/blog/wizard-agent-harness)** — dbt Developer Blog. dbt Labs describes Wizard, an agent built to work like an analytics engineer: it thinks in DAGs, tests and fixes its own work, and leaves you reviewing a working change instead of a first draft. *Why it matters:* a first-party look at how dbt Labs is designing AI assistance to fit the DAG-and-test workflow analytics engineers already use, rather than bolting on generic code generation.
- **[I built an S3, Snowflake, dbt and Airflow pipeline. Here is what broke, and why.](https://medium.com/@kracshan2000/i-built-an-s3-snowflake-dbt-and-airflow-pipeline-here-is-what-broke-and-why-a5575175a888?source=rss------dbt-5)** — Medium. A data engineer with a Microsoft Fabric/PySpark background documents what failed while learning the AWS/Snowflake/dbt/Airflow stack from scratch. *Why it matters:* a candid account of where this common stack combination actually trips up newcomers, useful for anyone onboarding a team onto it.
- **[What AI Copilots Get Wrong in a dbt Repo](https://npogeant.medium.com/what-ai-copilots-get-wrong-in-a-dbt-repo-c265b6eacbae?source=rss------dbt-5)** — Medium. Argues AI copilots in a dbt repo behave like a fast, capable junior who still needs the same review every junior's work gets. *Why it matters:* a grounded counterpoint to the assumption that AI-generated dbt models need less scrutiny than human-written ones.
