# Oracle deepens Snowflake and Google Cloud ties at Oracle AI World

The biggest story is Oracle's cloud-partnership push at Oracle AI World, expanding integrations with both Snowflake and Google Cloud. Beyond that, Snowflake published two substantive engineering write-ups — a governed MCP server for AI assistants and a native-table-to-Iceberg conversion playbook — and ClickHouse shared early benchmark results on agentic analytics.

## Top stories
- **[Snowflake and Oracle: Bringing the AI Data Cloud to OCI](https://www.snowflake.com/content/snowflake-site/global/en/blog/snowflake-oracle-partnership-ai-data-cloud-oci)** (alongside [Powering the agentic enterprise with latest innovations in Oracle AI Database@Google Cloud](https://cloud.google.com/blog/products/databases/oracle-ai-databasegoogle-cloud-whats-new-at-oracle-ai-world/)) — Snowflake Blog and Google Cloud. At Oracle AI World, Oracle expanded its partnership with Snowflake to bring the AI Data Cloud to Oracle Cloud Infrastructure as a native first-party service, while separately deepening its Google Cloud integration (including Google TPU access on OCI and tighter ties between Oracle AI Database, Gemini Enterprise and Google's Agent Development Kit). *Why it matters:* Oracle is positioning OCI as a neutral meeting point for rival data clouds rather than trying to replace them.
- **[The Agentic Analytics Benchmark: ranking tables and labeling failures with Jev](https://clickhouse.com/blog/agentic-analytics-benchmark-jev)** — ClickHouse Blog. Jev ranks warehouse tables and labels agent failures in under a second, but ClickHouse found that adding retrieval hints left accuracy flat across 20 models on its Agentic Analytics Benchmark. *Why it matters:* a rare negative result in the agent-tooling space, suggesting better retrieval hints alone won't fix agentic SQL accuracy.
- **[Snowflake's Managed MCP Server: Giving AI Assistants a Governed Door Into Your Warehouse (Part 1)](https://medium.com/snowflake/snowflakes-managed-mcp-server-giving-ai-assistants-a-governed-door-into-your-warehouse-part-1-09077cd30c61?source=rss----34b6daafc07---4)** and [Part 2](https://medium.com/snowflake/snowflakes-managed-mcp-server-building-a-chat-ui-part-2-2a2e9c85ecd2?source=rss----34b6daafc07---4) — Snowflake Engineering (Medium). A two-part walkthrough of Snowflake's managed MCP server — a contract letting clients like Claude or Cursor list and call warehouse tools behind existing RBAC — ending with a small chat UI built against it. *Why it matters:* a concrete, governance-first pattern for exposing a warehouse to AI assistants instead of handing out broad credentials.
- **[Converting Snowflake Native Tables to Iceberg: Part 2](https://medium.com/snowflake/converting-snowflake-native-tables-to-iceberg-part-2-76c3263188a8?source=rss----34b6daafc07---4)** — Snowflake Engineering (Medium). Covers the execution side of a side-by-side native-to-Iceberg conversion: data validation, a controlled rename-based cutover, and rollback. *Why it matters:* a detailed runbook for a migration pattern many Snowflake-on-Iceberg teams will need regardless of vendor.
