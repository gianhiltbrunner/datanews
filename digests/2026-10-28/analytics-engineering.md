# dbt 2.0's dbt-oss split and incremental-pipeline failure modes headline the week

Practitioners are still digesting dbt 2.0's licensing split between the proprietary Fusion build and the Apache-2.0 dbt-oss distribution, with one benchmark showing a sizeable parsing speedup. Elsewhere, two detailed write-ups covered the failure modes that show up once dbt pipelines and warehouse tables are relied on at real scale.

## Top stories
- **[UPDATE dbt 2.0: what changes when you upgrade, and the dbt vs dbt-oss split](https://www.reddit.com/r/DataBuildTool/comments/1xhis84/update_dbt_20_what_changes_when_you_upgrade_and/)** (with a companion benchmark, [dbt Core 2.0 Is Now dbt OSS: 5x Faster Parsing in My Test, and 8 YAML Habits That Break](https://medium.com/data-science-collective/dbt-core-2-0-is-now-dbt-oss-5x-faster-parsing-in-my-test-and-8-yaml-habits-that-break-4c7bf089a684?source=rss------dbt-5)) — r/DataBuildTool and Medium. `pip install dbt` now installs the proprietary Fusion build (full SQL comprehension, column-level lineage), while the Apache-2.0 distribution moved to `dbt-oss`; upgrading isn't just a version bump since v2 removes all deprecated functionality first. One tester clocked dbt-oss parsing a 2,000-model project roughly 5x faster than dbt-core 1.12. *Why it matters:* teams need to pick a distribution deliberately now, not just upgrade in place, and clear every deprecation warning before v2 will run at all.
- **[Reprocessing BigQuery at Scale: The Hidden Failure Modes of Incremental dbt Pipelines](https://medium.com/dbt-for-analytics/reprocessing-bigquery-at-scale-the-hidden-failure-modes-of-incremental-dbt-pipelines-b913cf3fd847?source=rss------dbt-5)** — Medium (dbt for Analytics). Covers designing deterministic backfills, safe partition replacement, and dependency-aware recovery for incremental dbt models in production. *Why it matters:* incremental models are a default choice for cost reasons, but their failure modes under reprocessing are rarely documented this concretely.
- **[A bank-filings warehouse built for reruns, duplicates and renames](https://medium.com/@smhassanraza1/a-bank-filings-warehouse-built-for-reruns-duplicates-and-renames-150fa7502547?source=rss------dbt-5)** — Medium. Walks through a pipeline that turns quarterly FDIC filings into tested BigQuery tables, built around the four failure cases that actually shaped its design. *Why it matters:* a grounded example of designing for idempotency and schema churn rather than bolting it on after an incident.
- **[The Most Expensive Data Pipeline Is the One Nobody Understands](https://medium.com/@pchandrika0613/the-most-expensive-data-pipeline-is-the-one-nobody-understands-6365282a464f?source=rss------analytics_engineering-5)** — Medium. Argues the real cost of undocumented dependencies and tribal knowledge is a data system only one person can fix. *Why it matters:* a reminder that documentation debt is a cost center, not just a nicety, for teams scaling analytics engineering practice.
