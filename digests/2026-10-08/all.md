# ASOS's Snowflake breach deepens as reports say AI agents keep skipping the semantic layer

Today's clearest throughline is governance catching up with AI: a deepening breach tied to ASOS's Snowflake instance shows what's at stake when access controls fail, while a cluster of reports argues AI agents mostly query raw tables instead of governed semantic layers, and BI vendors are shipping features to track AI-generated SQL rather than just run it. DuckDB continues to be the most active open-source story in data engineering proper, with a new view-only catalog mode and renewed interest in its DuckLake lakehouse format.
