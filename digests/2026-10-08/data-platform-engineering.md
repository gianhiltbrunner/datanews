# BBC: ASOS breach exposed more personal data than first disclosed

The window's most consequential story is the deepening ASOS breach tied to its Snowflake instance, which the BBC now reports exposed more personal data than ASOS first admitted. Alongside it, Databricks pushed two governance-flavored Unity Catalog and Apps features, and a ClickHouse benchmark claims a large cost advantage over BigQuery — a vendor-run number worth reading with that in mind. Much of the rest of the window is Snowflake-ticker stock chatter and keyword-matched noise with no data-platform content.
