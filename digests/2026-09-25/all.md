# Databricks buys Row Zero for Genie as Google Cloud ships an agentic database architecture

A thin day for newsletters and community discussion overall, but platform engineering had real news: Databricks' spreadsheet acquisition, Google Cloud's AlloyDB rework for AI-agent workloads, and a Delivery Hero case study on moving ad measurement from batch to streaming. Data engineering and analytics engineering were quieter, with DuckDB's Hugging Face integration and a dbt v2 migration guide as the standouts.

## Top 5 across domains
- **[Data Platform Engineering]** [Databricks Acquires Microsoft Excel Competitor Row Zero](https://news.google.com/rss/articles/CBMinAFBVV95cUxQU3gtTnRKU1FLTFg2UnhBYW5YR1RZb0hoN0NxOHZDU195TkJ3OEtLbk5WblBaVXJGeE9nUFdQYzcwUFJwWEN0cjVBUVQ0aFVOY3ZBLXJlUWZ4anlQTVBxMW1BNnNjUnhlRl9McnNPVTFYTXR4Ylk5OFowRmFnSmdXRmZyeXNzOFRIYkRTeElpVlY4QUVpcXdkVjNSclY?oc=5) — Databricks is buying spreadsheet startup Row Zero to bring live, governed spreadsheets into its Genie AI coworker product.
- **[Data Platform Engineering]** [AlloyDB delivers PostgreSQL for agents: Real-time data at agent scale, with full workload isolation](https://cloud.google.com/blog/products/databases/announcing-postgresql-for-agents-in-alloydb/) — Google Cloud's AlloyDB now provisions sandboxed, isolated database instances in seconds so bursts of AI agents don't overwhelm a mission-critical operational database.
- **[Data Platform Engineering]** [How Delivery Hero rebuilt real-time ad measurement with Apache Flink](https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/big-data/how-delivery-hero-rebuilt-real-time-ad-measurement-with-apache-flink/) — Moving from hourly batch to Flink cut event-to-record latency from 61 minutes to 1.2 seconds and monthly costs by about 57%.
- **[Data Engineering]** [DuckDB and Hugging Face: Querying Datasets Directly](https://duckdb.org/2026/09/25/hugging-face.html) — DuckDB can now query any Hugging Face-hosted dataset directly over its `hf://` protocol, without downloading it first.
