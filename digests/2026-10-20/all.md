# ClickHouse pits GPT-6 and Claude 5.5 models head-to-head on agentic analytics

A quiet day across all three domains, with feeds dominated by SEO tutorials, Snowflake stock-ticker chatter and keyword-collision noise. The clearest signal came from the data platform side: a cross-model agentic analytics benchmark, a practitioner's real cost breakdown of Snowflake's newly GA Postgres mirroring, and a hard migration deadline for a Microsoft Fabric integration, alongside a ClickHouse internals gotcha and a practitioner's take on warehouse design.

- **[Data Platform Engineering]** ClickHouse's [second-round Agentic Analytics Benchmark](https://clickhouse.com/blog/agentic-analytics-benchmark-round-two) added GPT-6, Claude Opus 5.5 and Claude Sonnet 5.5; all five models land within a point of each other, with Sonnet 5.5 shifting the most on turns, cost and truncated runs.
- **[Data Platform Engineering]** A Snowflake user [traced what Postgres mirroring actually bills for](https://www.reddit.com/r/snowflake/comments/1x9ud0b/i_dug_into_what_postgres_mirroring_actually_costs/) now that it's GA — per-mirror serverless apply tasks, delete-then-append sync, and a refresh-interval cost dial.
- **[Data Engineering]** A deep dive on [when ClickHouse merges change your query results](https://dev.to/maksim-gr/when-clickhouse-merges-change-your-query-results-5d99) explains how SummingMergeTree, AggregatingMergeTree and CollapsingMergeTree can return different results for the same query depending on merge timing.
- **[Data Platform Engineering]** Microsoft is [retiring the Fabric data agent custom tool in Foundry](https://community.fabric.microsoft.com/t5/Fabric-Updates-Blog/Retiring-the-Fabric-data-agent-custom-tool-in-Microsoft-Foundry/ba-p/5374418) on 11/6/2026, pushing teams toward a Fabric IQ-based replacement.
- **[Analytics Engineering]** A practitioner laid out [activity schemas, funnel tables and semantic layers as a preferred warehouse design](https://medium.com/@HarlanH/activity-schemas-funnel-tables-and-semantic-layers-my-preferred-data-warehouse-design-aa5896d74141?source=rss------analytics_engineering-5), built from years of business analytics work.
