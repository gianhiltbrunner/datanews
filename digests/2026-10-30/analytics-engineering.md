# BI vendors race to bolt semantic and ontology layers onto AI agents

Oracle and Zoho both expanded their platforms with semantic and ontology layers aimed at keeping AI agents from misreading business data, continuing a trend already visible across the lakehouse market this week. On the practitioner side, the best reads were a warning about a blind spot in dbt snapshots and an open-source tool for enforcing architectural rules on dbt projects.

- **[dbt Snapshots Have a Blind Spot: Late Files. It's Probably in Your Warehouse Right Now.](https://medium.com/@reliabledataengineering/dbt-snapshots-have-a-blind-spot-late-files-its-probably-in-your-warehouse-right-now-9c351191929c?source=rss------dbt-5)** — Medium. Standard dbt snapshots record history in arrival order, so a late-arriving file in a daily stream can silently corrupt the recorded history. *Why it matters:* A specific, checkable failure mode for a widely used dbt pattern that most teams assume just works.
