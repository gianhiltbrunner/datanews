# ClickHouse's 3,000-PR AI contributor and Oracle's Snowflake tie-up headline the day

ClickHouse disclosed that an autonomous agent has merged thousands of pull requests into its codebase, while Oracle deepened its Snowflake partnership on OCI and Google Cloud let BigQuery query Databricks' Iceberg tables with no data copying. Separately, Oracle and Zoho both bolted semantic and ontology layers onto their platforms to keep AI agents from misreading business data, and data engineers traded practical field notes on running Iceberg and Delta Lake in production.

## Top 5 across domains
- **[Data Platform Engineering]** ClickHouse introduced GroeneAI, an autonomous engineering agent that has contributed more than 3,000 merged pull requests to the project in under seven months.
- **[Data Platform Engineering]** Oracle deepened its OCI partnership with Snowflake, bringing Snowflake's AI Data Cloud onto Oracle Cloud Infrastructure as a first-party service.
- **[Analytics Engineering]** Oracle and Zoho both added semantic or ontology layers to their platforms this week, aimed at stopping AI agents from misreading business data — a sign the feature is becoming table stakes.
- **[Data Platform Engineering]** Google Cloud's borderless Lakehouse now lets BigQuery query Databricks-managed Apache Iceberg tables directly, zero-copy, cutting a common source of duplicated pipelines and cost.
- **[Data Engineering]** Practitioners published detailed field notes on keeping Apache Iceberg tables fast and cheap in production and on using Delta Lake's Change Data Feed to replace full-table rewrites with incremental MERGE.
