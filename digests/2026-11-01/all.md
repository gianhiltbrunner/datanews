# AWS capacity crunch, dbt Labs' agent-skills verdict, and Databricks' CI/CD database branching

The biggest story spans every domain: AWS says committed cloud capacity is largely sold out through 2027. dbt Labs published a rare self-critical benchmark on whether coding agents still need bespoke dbt skills, and Databricks shared concrete CI/CD lessons from branching production-scale Postgres databases. Snowflake's $300 million Saudi Arabia investment rounds out a day heavier on infrastructure and research than on tooling releases.
