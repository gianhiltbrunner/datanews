# dbt v2 ships as dbt Labs and Fivetran unveil merged data stack

dbt-core crossed into v2.0 this week and was quickly patched to v2.0.5, landing right alongside a wave of wire coverage about dbt Labs and Fivetran unveiling joint "agent-ready" capabilities at dbt Summit 2026 in Las Vegas. Elsewhere, a candid write-up of giving an AI agent commit access to a real dbt project, a case for treating the semantic layer as warehouse scaffolding, and steady release trickle from Cube and Lightdash rounded out the week.

## Top stories
- **[dbt 2.0.0 ("Benjamin Franklin")](https://github.com/dbt-labs/dbt/releases/tag/v2.0.0)** — dbt-core releases, via Google News. dbt-core's first major version in years adds installable agent skills (`SKILL.md` directories) from packages, native Databricks metric-view materializations, and expanded ClickHouse connection settings; it was patched two days later in [v2.0.5](https://github.com/dbt-labs/dbt/releases/tag/v2.0.5) to fix a BigQuery ADBC edge case that could wrongly replace instead of merge incremental tables. The release lands alongside a cluster of wire stories describing dbt Labs and Fivetran — reported as newly merged — debuting dbt State and new enterprise capabilities at dbt Summit 2026. *Why it matters:* a major-version bump to the industry's default transformation tool, timed to a combined dbt Labs/Fivetran go-to-market push, is worth tracking for how "agent-ready" features actually show up in projects.
- **[I Gave an AI Agent Write Access to My dbt Project for a Week. Here's What It Got Wrong.](https://medium.com/tech-with-abhishek/i-gave-an-ai-agent-write-access-to-my-dbt-project-for-a-week-heres-what-it-got-wrong-8e1c4c242ed4?source=rss------dbt-5)** — Medium. A hands-on log of running an AI agent ("dbt Wizard") against a real project, cataloguing which calls it got right and where it went wrong. *Why it matters:* a concrete data point in the live debate over how much autonomy to hand AI agents inside production analytics code.

## Releases & tools
- **[Cube 1.7.42](https://github.com/cube-js/cube/releases/tag/v1.7.42)** — the latest of three releases this week; the prior two cleared 52 Dependabot alerts, replaced the CVE-flagged `extract-zip` dependency, upgraded to Angular 20 LTS, and migrated the DuckDB driver to DuckDB 1.5.5.
- **[Lightdash 2.269.0](https://github.com/lightdash/lightdash/releases/tag/2.269.0)** — three releases rounded out a new "documents" feature: viewing/downloading documents as code, duplicating them into a space, and marking favorites.
