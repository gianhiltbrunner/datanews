# dbt v2 and Databricks' gray-failure detector lead a thin day for pipelines

A light day for original reporting: data-engineering and data-platform feeds were dominated by SEO content and stock-ticker noise, but a few substantive stories cut through. dbt-core crossed into v2.0 as dbt Labs and Fivetran touted a merged, agent-ready data stack at dbt Summit 2026, Databricks detailed an anomaly detector for outages that hide from normal monitoring, and both Google Cloud and Snowflake shipped ways to reach data across clouds and catalogs without copying it.

## Top 5 across domains
- **[Analytics Engineering]** [dbt 2.0.0 ("Benjamin Franklin")](https://github.com/dbt-labs/dbt/releases/tag/v2.0.0) — dbt-core's first major version in years, patched to v2.0.5 within days, landed alongside wire coverage of dbt Labs and Fivetran unveiling a merged, agent-ready data stack at dbt Summit 2026.
- **[Data Platform Engineering]** [RADAR: Catch gray failures with anomaly detection](https://www.databricks.com/blog/radar-catch-gray-failures-anomaly-detection) — Databricks' four-stage anomaly-detection pipeline cut incident-discovery time by 95% for partial outages that never trip conventional monitoring.
- **[Data Platform Engineering]** [Accelerating the borderless Lakehouse: cross-cloud caching preview](https://cloud.google.com/blog/products/data-analytics/borderless-lakehouse-cross-cloud-caching-and-connections/) — Google Cloud previewed caching that lets query engines and AI agents reach governed data across S3, ADLS and other clouds without copying it into BigQuery first.
- **[Data Engineering]** [Persistent Databases in the Browser with DuckDB-Wasm and OPFS](https://duckdb.org/2026/09/18/opfs-wasm) — DuckDB-Wasm can now persist to disk in the browser via the Origin Private File System, ending the "vanishes when the tab closes" limitation from its 2021 launch.
- **[Analytics Engineering]** [I Gave an AI Agent Write Access to My dbt Project for a Week. Here's What It Got Wrong.](https://medium.com/tech-with-abhishek/i-gave-an-ai-agent-write-access-to-my-dbt-project-for-a-week-heres-what-it-got-wrong-8e1c4c242ed4?source=rss------dbt-5) — a hands-on account of the good and bad calls an AI agent made with commit access to a real dbt project.
