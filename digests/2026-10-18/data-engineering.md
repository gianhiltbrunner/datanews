# Practitioners dig into ClickHouse internals and Postgres' vector scaling limits

It's a quiet day for breaking news in data engineering, but several deep-dive technical write-ups stand out: a walkthrough of ClickHouse's insert-to-merge lifecycle, a look at where pgvector's HNSW index breaks down at scale, and a runbook for safely downscaling a production OpenSearch cluster. No notable tool releases cleared the bar today.

## Top stories
- **[Follow one row: what ClickHouse does with your INSERT](https://dev.to/maksim-gr/follow-one-row-what-clickhouse-does-with-your-insert-g83)** — DEV. A data infrastructure engineer traces a single row from INSERT through merging, deduplication and deletion to explain recurring confusion around `ReplicatedReplacingMergeTree`, stale reads and "Too many parts" errors. *Why it matters:* these are some of the most common ClickHouse operational questions, and few explainers walk the full lifecycle in one place.
- **[The 'Just Use Postgres' Vector Cliff: Where pgvector HNSW Breaks at 50M Rows](https://dev.to/asifmiqbal/the-just-use-postgres-vector-cliff-where-pgvector-hnsw-breaks-at-50m-rows-417m)** — DEV. Describes how unpartitioned pgvector HNSW indexes need roughly 2x raw vector size in RAM and start failing on write amplification, VACUUM stalls and filtered-search recall once collections reach 20M–50M embeddings. *Why it matters:* teams defaulting to "just use Postgres" for vector search need a concrete scaling ceiling to plan around.
- **[How I Downscaled a Production OpenSearch Cluster Without Losing Data (or My Sanity)](https://medium.com/towards-data-engineering/how-i-downscaled-a-production-opensearch-cluster-without-losing-data-or-my-sanity-08ee8e84ac7e?source=rss------data_engineering-5)** — Medium. A step-by-step runbook for shrinking Kubernetes-backed OpenSearch StatefulSets without data loss. *Why it matters:* downscaling stateful clusters safely is a recurring, high-stakes operational task with little good documentation.
