# Oracle's semantic layer push for AI agents leads a vendor-heavy news day

Oracle announced a business semantic layer, AI planning engine and AI catalog for its Autonomous AI Lakehouse, and separately deepened its OCI partnership with Snowflake — two different Oracle stories that both ran wide across the wires. On the engineering side, Snowflake's Optima Planning optimizer cut a production query from two hours to 30 seconds, Airbnb's Chronon platform slashed recommendation staleness to under a minute, and dbt Labs' rename of dbt Core to dbt OSS came with an independent benchmark showing 5x faster parsing.

## Top 5 across domains
- **[Analytics Engineering]** Oracle announced a business semantic layer, AI planning engine and AI catalog for its Autonomous AI Lakehouse, framed as stopping AI agents from misreading business data, with expanded Apache Iceberg support — covered by over a dozen outlets.
- **[Data Platform Engineering]** Snowflake's Optima Planning optimizer now learns across queries instead of just repeats of the same one, cutting a production 19-join analytics query from almost two hours to 30 seconds with no manual tuning.
- **[Data Engineering]** Airbnb extended its Chronon feature platform with Push Mode and Near Real-Time Model Transform, cutting recommendation embedding staleness from nearly two days to under a minute.
- **[Analytics Engineering]** dbt Core is now branded dbt OSS and stays Apache-2.0 after the Fivetran merger; an independent 2,000-model benchmark found roughly 5x faster parsing than dbt-core 1.12.
- **[Data Platform Engineering]** Oracle and Snowflake deepened their native AI and data services partnership on OCI, while Snowflake's CEO publicly questioned Databricks's reported 80% growth rate and Databricks's CEO hinted at AI cooperation between the two.
