# Snowflake's cross-query optimizer turns a 2-hour query into 30 seconds

Snowflake's Optima Planning engine picked up cross-query learning, cutting a production 19-join query from nearly two hours to 30 seconds with no manual tuning. Oracle and Snowflake deepened their partnership on OCI, while Snowflake's CEO and Databricks' CEO traded public claims about growth and AI strategy. Microsoft also made its OneLake governance tooling generally available, and several cloud providers shipped smaller reliability and cost features.

## Top stories
- **[Snowflake Optima Planning: Cross-Query Performance](https://medium.com/snowflake/snowflake-optima-planning-cross-query-performance-1dda1871346d?source=rss----34b6daafc07---4)** — Snowflake Engineering (Medium). Optima Planning now learns across queries, not just from repeats of the same query: when a new query shares a plan fragment or join with one that already ran, it reuses that feedback on the first execution. In production at a large US healthcare technology company, this cut a 19-join analytics query from almost two hours to 30 seconds. *Why it matters:* a rare concrete, quantified example of automatic query optimization delivering an order-of-magnitude win with no manual tuning.
