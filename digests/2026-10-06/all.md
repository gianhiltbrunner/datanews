# A suspected Snowflake-linked breach rattles ASOS, while data teams trade incident post-mortems

The biggest story of the window was ASOS customers receiving a "hacked" push notification referencing the retailer's Snowflake instance, sending both companies' shares down. Data engineering and data platform engineering both produced strong incident and architecture write-ups — a dbt model that silently erased finance data, and Databricks' and Amagi's accounts of running dbt and graph databases at real scale — while analytics engineering had a quiet day led mainly by a data-quality survey stat.
