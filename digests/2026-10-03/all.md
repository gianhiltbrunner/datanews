# Uber Eats Halves Search Latency as Spanner Gets Native Transactional Queues

Real-world engineering wins led the day: Uber cut Eats search latency in half with a pipeline rework, and Google Cloud gave Spanner native transactional messaging for agentic workloads. Cloudflare's Basin analytics platform reached general availability, Snowflake published a guide for migrating native tables to Iceberg, and dbt practitioners got a first look at moving from dbt Core v1 to the Rust-based dbt v2.
