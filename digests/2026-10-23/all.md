# Real-team write-ups outshine a noisy day of stock chatter and vendor press

Across data engineering, analytics engineering and data platform engineering, the standout work came from practitioners describing what actually runs in production: Netflix's container isolation, Snowflake's guardrails for unattended coding agents, and Databricks' async streaming GA. Vendor press releases, Snowflake stock commentary and AI-partnership announcements otherwise dominated the feeds, with dbt's metric-standard fragmentation the lone piece sparking real discussion in analytics engineering.

## Top 5 across domains
- **[Data Platform Engineering]** **[Your Agent Ran Overnight. Here Is What It Got Wrong.](https://medium.com/snowflake/your-agent-ran-overnight-here-is-what-it-got-wrong-98a8117e466a?source=rss----34b6daafc07---4)** — Snowflake Engineering walks through the architecture and six failure modes behind running unattended coding agents against production systems.
- **[Data Engineering]** **[Single-tenant Titus: VM-level isolation on a multi-tenant container platform](https://netflixtechblog.com/single-tenant-titus-vm-level-isolation-on-a-multi-tenant-container-platform-6d8b1909d163?source=rss----2615bd06b42e---4)** — Netflix explains how its Kubernetes-based Titus platform gives individual tenants dedicated isolation guarantees without each team owning a cluster.
- **[Data Platform Engineering]** **[Python Asynchronous TransformWithState for Streaming is now GA](https://www.reddit.com/r/databricks/comments/1xcznm3/python_asynchronous_transformwithstate_for/)** — Databricks' new async Structured Streaming API batches state-store operations across keys for up to 3x throughput.
