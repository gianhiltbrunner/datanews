# Netflix details VM-level isolation for its multi-tenant Titus platform

Netflix's writeup on isolating tenants inside its Titus container platform was the clearest piece of engineering writing in an otherwise thin, noise-heavy day. The rest of the DuckDB 2.0 launch's aftershocks played out in community threads rather than new news, and a handful of DEV and Medium posts offered solid field notes on Iceberg maintenance, messy JSON, and LLMs on company data.

## Top stories
- **[Single-tenant Titus: VM-level isolation on a multi-tenant container platform](https://netflixtechblog.com/single-tenant-titus-vm-level-isolation-on-a-multi-tenant-container-platform-6d8b1909d163?source=rss----2615bd06b42e---4)** — Netflix Tech Blog. Netflix explains how Titus, its Kubernetes-based container platform that launches millions of containers for internal teams, adds VM-level isolation so a tenant gets dedicated guarantees without owning its own cluster. *Why it matters:* the same tradeoff — shared platform efficiency versus per-tenant isolation guarantees — shows up in any team's shared pipeline or streaming infrastructure.
