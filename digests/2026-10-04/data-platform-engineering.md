# Cloudflare publishes Basin pricing, claiming it undercuts AWS Athena by 50%

A noisy day for data-platform-engineering feeds — most "Snowflake" matches were high-school sports and stock coverage rather than engineering news — but two real items surfaced: pricing details for Cloudflare's Basin analytics platform, and a legal update on the Snowflake-linked breach wave.

## Top stories
- **[Cloudflare Basin GA: $2.50/TB Beats AWS Athena 50%](https://news.google.com/rss/articles/CBMie0FVX3lxTFBOTmZQTENUMWFlRzN3QlYzcExVYm5hZE5VX1RMOWZzVUdCY0dLbkNDcnNwWTBJd1VNLWwtYWVEQnc5WTZUdWJXSWpJeGthSDhTMlNabzJ5LVRCMUZxS0dPS0I0UEZmM2RCZVh4ZlBtd29tLXFYRnlNSHIyNA?oc=5)** — shattered.io (via Google News). Cloudflare's Basin analytics platform is now priced at $2.50 per TB scanned, a figure the piece says undercuts AWS Athena by roughly half. *Why it matters:* a credible low-cost entrant into serverless SQL-on-object-storage querying puts price pressure on established Athena/BigQuery-style services.
- **[Hacker Linked to Ticketmaster Snowflake Breach Pleads Guilty in 165-Company Attack](https://news.google.com/rss/articles/CBMiugFBVV95cUxPdGJiLWpwS2RrN0oyNDhmWDZ4YTg5c2FyMmlkelFiVDhINDVhLUNqWFZ2ODhPaS1IbkxOdmNxaTlGazhnNVA3MFVkU0tRUzROR3IwQTBfNjVscHZDa1lXbWZna2FrVExLOFVzbWRwel9HOTRBaDExOGZ6dmI1YlJNMDdQNVcxS2l2UTkyVjAtenpOYl9kTHJMb2Z3TzRMTUxiV2ZnOGIzSmxOWXh4SENuWlBia1phb2U1dHc?oc=5)** — TicketNews (via Google News). A hacker tied to the 2024 wave of breaches against Snowflake customer accounts, including Ticketmaster, pleaded guilty to charges covering attacks on 165 companies. *Why it matters:* a concrete legal outcome from the incident that pushed many platform teams to tighten credential and MFA practices on warehouse accounts.
