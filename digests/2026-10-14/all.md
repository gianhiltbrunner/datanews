# Redshift closes the Iceberg concurrent-write gap as Dell courts AI agents with a data platform push

A platform-engineering-heavy day: AWS removed a long-standing multi-writer pain point for Iceberg on Redshift, and ClickHouse published a detailed tail-latency incident write-up. Dell's expanded AI Data Platform drew wide (if duplicated) coverage as vendors keep racing to make enterprise data legible to agents, while data engineering was otherwise quiet apart from one sharp debugging post on a timezone bug in Athena.
