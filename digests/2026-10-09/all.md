# Platforms race to wire AI agents into data infrastructure, from Snowflake's gateway to Dell's semantic layer

Across all three domains, the throughline is vendors building governance and context layers for AI agents touching enterprise data — Snowflake's Cortex AI Gateway, Dell's semantic layer, and Databricks' agent-friendly branching databases — even as one widely-shared story argues most agents still skip semantic layers entirely. Real engineering write-ups from Netflix, Spotify and a ClickHouse migration case study provided the day's substantive technical reading, and Snowflake also had to publicly distance itself from a customer breach.
