# Databricks tackles catalog lock-in as Snowflake, Cloudflare duel on pricing

Databricks previewed Unity Catalog APIs built to stop catalog lock-in, while Snowflake and Cloudflare kept pushing on AI and analytics cost with a new caching GA and a fresh Basin-vs-Snowflake pricing comparison. Elsewhere, the data-engineering community dug into why AWS bought DuckDB Labs over Polars and traded incident stories about pipelines that fail silently, while analytics engineering had a quiet day dominated by an unverified claim about dbt-core's future.

## Top 5 across domains
- **[Data Platform Engineering]** **[Unlocking Data Portability: Preventing Catalog Lock-in with REGISTER and UNREGISTER APIs](https://www.databricks.com/blog/unlocking-data-portability-preventing-catalog-lock-register-and-unregister-apis)** — Databricks Blog. New Unity Catalog endpoints let a table move between Iceberg REST Catalogs via a metadata handoff, with no data copying and no split-brain ownership risk; currently in private preview.
- **[Data Engineering]** **[AWS acquires DuckDB Labs. Not Polars.](https://dataengineeringcentral.substack.com/p/aws-acquires-duckdb-labs-not-polars)** — Data Engineering Central. A community analysis of why AWS's acquisition landed on DuckDB Labs rather than Polars, and what that signals about corporate backing of open-source embedded engines.
- **[Data Platform Engineering]** **[Response Caching for AI_COMPLETE now GA](https://www.reddit.com/r/snowflake/comments/1wy5ww9/response_caching_for_ai_complete_now_ga/)** — Snowflake (via r/snowflake). Response caching for AI_COMPLETE is now generally available, cutting inference costs on repeated or near-duplicate row content.
- **[Data Engineering]** **[A cache key ate 99.9% of my records and the pipeline looked green](https://dev.to/kyien/a-cache-key-ate-999-of-my-records-and-the-pipeline-looked-green-37i0)** — DEV. A cache-key bug silently dropped 99.9% of records while every job metric still showed healthy, because monitoring tracked runs rather than rows.
- **[Analytics Engineering]** **[The Hidden Cost of dbt 2.0's Proprietary Turn](https://medium.com/@m.awais1060/the-hidden-cost-of-dbt-2-0s-proprietary-turn-d4c939e9479b?source=rss------dbt-5)** — Medium. An opinion piece argues dbt's default install has turned proprietary and dbt-core is being deprecated, with a limited window left to migrate — a claim worth watching for confirmation from dbt Labs.
