# Databricks tackles catalog lock-in as Snowflake, Cloudflare duel on pricing

Databricks previewed new Unity Catalog APIs aimed squarely at catalog lock-in, Snowflake shipped a cost-cutting cache for its AI functions, and a fresh comparison piece kept the Cloudflare Basin-vs-Snowflake pricing story going. Most of the rest of the window was stock commentary and clearly mismatched Google News hits (local weather, an earthquake report, a puppy rescue) pulled in by keyword overlap rather than actual platform-engineering content.

## Top stories
- **[Unlocking Data Portability: Preventing Catalog Lock-in with REGISTER and UNREGISTER APIs](https://www.databricks.com/blog/unlocking-data-portability-preventing-catalog-lock-register-and-unregister-apis)** — Databricks Blog. New Unity Catalog REGISTER/UNREGISTER endpoints let a table move between Iceberg REST Catalogs by handing off its existing metadata location — no data copying — while guaranteeing exactly one catalog owns the table at a time, avoiding "split-brain" ownership; the capability is in private preview. *Why it matters:* it's a concrete, vendor-backed answer to a real lakehouse pain point — being stuck with one catalog once you've registered tables there.
- **[Response Caching for AI_COMPLETE now GA](https://www.reddit.com/r/snowflake/comments/1wy5ww9/response_caching_for_ai_complete_now_ga/)** — r/snowflake (Snowflake staff post). Response caching for the AI_COMPLETE function is now generally available, so repeated or near-duplicate inputs (e.g. canned support replies, repeated product descriptions) no longer pay for inference on every row. *Why it matters:* a direct, measurable cost lever for teams running AI functions over tables with repetitive content.
- **[Cloudflare Basin vs Snowflake: $0.06/GB Analytics [2026]](https://news.google.com/rss/articles/CBMihAFBVV95cUxNdDRqMVBOamFEeGFnQmhwRTZQYmtETHZUY0JCWEVYRTBlN3ZLSXhtVHlMZnFlcFlUYm43MVg1c1h3dGM4bmxKS2pWb2NleHNBUmhQMnNSV0I4NmR0d1RsWGdHeXNzWmt6R2RaeEFmNERwSjVORjlqNF95RzFpMkd3NFVXZks?oc=5)** — tech-insider.org (Google News). A new comparison puts Cloudflare Basin's analytics pricing against Snowflake in per-GB terms. *Why it matters:* keeps the Basin pricing-disruption story — which broke this week — moving, this time framed against Snowflake rather than AWS Athena.
