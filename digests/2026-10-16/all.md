# Atlassian's security lakehouse and Snowflake's Postgres mirroring headline a vendor-engineering day

Today's strongest stories came from platform teams and vendors documenting real architecture rather than from breaking news: Atlassian detailed the OCSF-based security lakehouse it runs on Databricks, and Snowflake shipped automatic Postgres-to-Snowflake data mirroring. On the analytics engineering side, dbt Labs pushed the industry conversation toward keeping AI agents on an open data stack while detailing the DAG-native agent it built internally. Data engineering was the quietest domain of the three, with DuckDB's own benchmark of its in-development v2.0 build the clearest signal in an otherwise thin feed.

## Top 5 across domains
- **[Data Platform Engineering]** [Reducing the noise: How Atlassian built an OCSF security lakehouse on Databricks](https://www.databricks.com/blog/reducing-noise-how-atlassian-built-ocsf-security-lakehouse-databricks) — Atlassian consolidated two decades of security logging at global scale into a standardized lakehouse, a concrete answer to a problem most long-lived logging setups eventually hit.
- **[Data Platform Engineering]** [Data Mirroring Is GA: Replicate Postgres Data to Snowflake in Seconds](https://www.snowflake.com/content/snowflake-site/global/en/blog/data-mirroring-ga-snowflake-postgres) — automatic replication of inserts, updates, deletes and schema changes removes a whole category of CDC pipeline work for teams running Postgres alongside Snowflake.
- **[Analytics Engineering]** [Open Data Infrastructure in the age of AI agents](https://roundup.getdbt.com/p/open-data-infrastructure-in-the-age) — dbt Labs, Fivetran and a16z voices make the case for building your own AI analyst instead of wiring agents straight into SaaS tools, keeping the data stack open.
- **[Data Engineering]** [Benchmarking DuckDB v2.0-dev on NVIDIA Vera](https://duckdb.org/2026/10/16/nvidia-vera-benchmark.html) — DuckDB ran the full TPC-H benchmark at scale factor 1,000 on NVIDIA's new Vera CPU, an early performance signal ahead of the v2.0 release.
- **[Analytics Engineering]** [Inside the dbt Wizard harness: building an agent that thinks in DAGs](https://docs.getdbt.com/blog/wizard-agent-harness) — dbt Labs explains how its own AI agent reasons in DAGs and tests its own work, a concrete look at agent design built for analytics engineering rather than bolted on.
