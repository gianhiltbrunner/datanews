# dbt Labs makes the case for keeping AI agents on an open data stack

The dbt ecosystem's biggest talking point was about AI agents rather than SQL: dbt Labs' roundup gathered Fivetran, dbt Labs and a16z voices on why companies are building their own AI analysts instead of wiring agents straight into SaaS tools, and dbt's developer blog detailed the DAG-native agent it built internally. On the release side, dbt-core pushed out v2.0.8 while continuing to promote v2 availability from the v1 line, and a Medium piece surveyed a fast-consolidating orchestration market.

## Top stories
- **[Open Data Infrastructure in the age of AI agents](https://roundup.getdbt.com/p/open-data-infrastructure-in-the-age)** — The Analytics Engineering Roundup (dbt Labs). George Fraser, Tristan Handy and a16z's Martin Casado discuss why companies are building their own AI analysts rather than connecting agents straight to SaaS tools. *Why it matters:* a case from industry figures for keeping the data stack open as agents become the primary consumer of warehouse data.
- **[Inside the dbt Wizard harness: building an agent that thinks in DAGs](https://docs.getdbt.com/blog/wizard-agent-harness)** — dbt Developer Blog. dbt Labs explains how its Wizard agent is built to reason in DAGs, test and fix its own work, and leave you reviewing a working change rather than a first draft. *Why it matters:* a concrete look at how a transformation vendor is designing agents to fit analytics engineering workflows specifically, rather than bolting on a generic coding assistant.
- **[Cron is not an orchestrator. Here's how the lakehouse actually gets run](https://medium.com/@enginkosure/cron-is-not-an-orchestrator-heres-how-the-lakehouse-actually-gets-run-c65cf4dcf291?source=rss------dbt-5)** — Medium. A tour of a consolidating orchestration landscape, touching Airflow 3.3, dbt v2's Rust engine, and reported moves by Prefect and Fivetran to absorb competitors. *Why it matters:* argues the tooling layer under dbt is changing shape quickly, which matters for anyone picking an orchestrator today.

## Releases & tools
- **[dbt-core v2.0.8](https://github.com/dbt-labs/dbt/releases/tag/v2.0.8)** — the latest in a run of releases (also v1.12.6, v1.11.16) that lowers the minimum supported Python version to 3.10, fixes `dbt build --write-catalog` silently skipping `catalog.json`, and keeps promoting dbt v2 availability from the v1 CLI.
