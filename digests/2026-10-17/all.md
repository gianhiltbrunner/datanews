# Amazon acquires DuckLabs, bringing DuckDB's team into AWS

The day's defining story is Amazon's acquisition of DuckLabs, the company behind DuckDB, which now gets its first integration into Amazon Aurora PostgreSQL. Warehouse engines also moved forward on two fronts — Databricks made multi-statement transactions GA and Snowflake's Adaptive Warehouses cut DML costs by up to 13.7x — while dbt Labs made the case for keeping data infrastructure open as AI agents take on more analysis.

## Top 5 across domains
- **[Data Engineering]** **[Amazon Acquires DuckLabs and Integrates DuckDB in Amazon Aurora PostgreSQL](https://www.infoq.com/news/2026/10/amazon-acquires-ducklabs/?utm_campaign=infoq_content&utm_source=infoq&utm_medium=feed&utm_term=AI%2C+ML+%26+Data+Engineering)** — Amazon has acquired DuckLabs; DuckDB's co-founders stay on to lead the team under AWS, which says DuckDB will remain open source.
- **[Data Platform Engineering]** **[Transactions are Generally Available, advancing warehousing on the Lakehouse](https://www.databricks.com/blog/transactions-are-generally-available-advancing-warehousing-lakehouse)** — Multi-Statement Transactions are now GA on the Databricks Lakehouse.
