# Google Cloud outage hits major banks as Databricks and DuckDB both ship big updates

The day's biggest story was infrastructure, not a product launch: a Google Cloud outage knocked services at HSBC, Revolut, Lloyds and Equifax offline. Elsewhere, Databricks introduced agent memory, DuckDB confirmed its 2.0 release lands tomorrow, a years-old Snowflake breach reached a guilty plea, and a dbt practitioner's planted-bug experiment showed a green test suite can still hide lost rows.

## Top 5 across domains
- **[Data Platform Engineering]** A [Google Cloud outage took down digital services](https://news.google.com/rss/articles/CBMirgFBVV95cUxQbkpKMVJTenVXYWVLcGpDclFmd3ptb3VhSXh2dGtzU2lLbWtRYUdocFU1SHlMNTliTndlRTNUZXF1T3RENm1IYTFrX1VPS3R2VGhMSUwxSS1kRkFLWENLdEFxTVNzVWJuOXNtQ01QaEVRM2l3WmYxSmh3TWZsSXRuakJMd1JQblhOek4wbUxJcXNnaXpYd2UyenRlb2ZzV0ZEUFN5b2kzWkNsc0dWQ3c?oc=5) at HSBC, Revolut, Bank of Scotland, Lloyds and Equifax, among others.
- **[Data Platform Engineering]** Databricks [introduced agent memory](https://www.databricks.com/blog/introducing-agent-memory-databricks) on Agent Bricks, with a companion post on designing persistent memory ([MemCurrent](https://www.databricks.com/blog/memcurrent-designing-persistent-memory-production-agents)) for production agents.
