# DuckDB 2.0 lands tomorrow as acquisitions reshape ingestion tooling

A thin day for substantive data engineering writing — the feeds were dominated by recruitment posts and SEO-driven tool promos — but DuckDB's next major version is imminent and the ingestion-tool M&A wave continues.

## Top stories
- **[DuckDB 2.0 is coming tomorrow!](https://bsky.app/profile/duckdb.org/post/3mycnrfcpms22)** — DuckDB (Bluesky). The DuckDB team announced that version 2.0 ships the next day, without further detail in the post itself. *Why it matters:* a major version bump from the project driving much of the current embedded-OLAP momentum is worth watching for breaking changes.
- **[Helium Ventures Acquires Jitsu And Expands Self-Hosting Product Portfolio](https://news.google.com/rss/articles/CBMimAFBVV95cUxNa1dWMGtyaVRaVHFSREsydWQ4RHNkOHdVXzJJNTBtZldEOGVwWThoWnEwRHlNYWhmeWNfaFpWcTU5X3NOTEZjZmQ3Qk5rZEh4Vm83Vl9GWUxLeGVmTDI0RzVObFZRR19UOWVnRUIwclZfRnp3VjFhOEUzZ3hpd3lBN1E0RFJ5NF84ZmNKbnR4YWlBRXVtbWlIXw?oc=5)** — Pulse 2.0. Helium Ventures acquired the open-source event-ingestion tool Jitsu to grow a self-hosting product line. *Why it matters:* another consolidation move among smaller ingestion/CDC vendors, following similar recent deals in the space.

## Worth reading
