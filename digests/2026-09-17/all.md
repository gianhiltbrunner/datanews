# dbt v2.0 goes GA as ClickHouse joins the dbt platform and Databricks bets $350M on Singapore

dbt Labs shipped a complete rewrite of its engine as dbt v2.0, general availability, renamed from dbt Core, and ClickHouse used the moment to launch its own dbt v2 adapter in public beta. Databricks backed up its AI-driven growth story with a concrete $350M Singapore expansion, while Dropbox and Snowflake published detailed accounts of retrofitting a content platform for AI and benchmarking real-time analytics respectively.

## Top 5 across domains
- **[Analytics Engineering]** [dbt v2.0 is GA](https://docs.getdbt.com/blog/dbt-v2-is-ga) — a complete rewrite of the Fusion engine, renamed simply "dbt," billed as the foundation for the next decade of the project.
- **[Data Platform Engineering]** [ClickHouse is now available on the dbt platform](https://clickhouse.com/blog/clickhouse-is-now-available-on-the-dbt-platform) — its dbt v2 adapter, powered by dbt's new Rust engine, launches in public beta on day one of the v2.0 GA.
