# ClickHouse joins the dbt platform as Databricks puts $350M behind a Singapore bet

ClickHouse became available on the dbt platform via a public-beta Rust-engine adapter, Databricks announced a $350M Singapore expansion that more than doubles its local workforce, and Snowflake's engineering team published a repeatable benchmark for sub-second real-time analytics. Outside of that, the Google News feeds were saturated with routine Snowflake executive stock-sale filings, which we've left out.

## Top stories
- **[ClickHouse is now available on the dbt platform](https://clickhouse.com/blog/clickhouse-is-now-available-on-the-dbt-platform)** — ClickHouse Blog. The ClickHouse adapter for dbt v2 is in public beta, powered by dbt's new Rust engine, and ClickHouse itself is joining the dbt platform in private beta for both open-source ClickHouse and ClickHouse Cloud. *Why it matters:* it plugs ClickHouse directly into dbt's new v2.0 engine on day one of the GA launch.
- **[Databricks Bets $350M on Singapore as It More Than Doubles Local Workforce](https://news.google.com/rss/articles/CBMiigFBVV95cUxNV3dQbjI3QXVLTjJ4Rm1ENEgzRkMwN1FZdWtNRkR4RVZJNFZqQi15UHl2Sm8ydHZoVk5BUWtydzJXbDJrNDNZd0ZUSjZ6OUZFNTdzTVQzN0x2S3F4bGlIR1JCZkZHZjBaamQtblR6Y09MTnpvdGlhNHJ5UUw2MDZRb3NfMGJsc05uWnc?oc=5)** — Google News (TechRepublic). Databricks is investing over $350M and more than doubling its Singapore workforce, framed as a bet on APAC AI-driven demand. *Why it matters:* another concrete signal of how much of Databricks' growth is being redirected into regional capacity.
- **[Real-Time Analytics on Snowflake: A Repeatable Benchmark for Sub-Second Latency at Scale](https://medium.com/snowflake/real-time-analytics-on-snowflake-a-repeatable-benchmark-for-sub-second-latency-at-scale-49b666f2deb2?source=rss----34b6daafc07---4)** — Snowflake Engineering (Medium). An engineer lays out a repeatable benchmark methodology for sub-second Snowflake queries, framed against the trade-offs of bolting on a separate real-time engine like ClickHouse or a Hadoop-era stitched-together stack. *Why it matters:* a technical, opinionated take on the entitlement and data-movement costs of a multi-engine real-time architecture.

- **[ClickHouse v26.10.1.1](https://github.com/ClickHouse/ClickHouse/releases/tag/v26.10.1.1-new)** — the newest of several ClickHouse releases in the window, including stable and LTS patch builds.
- **[Retirement Update: SAP container images removed October 14, 2026](https://azure.microsoft.com/updates?id=571342)** — Azure Updates. The containerized SAP data connector, already retired since September 14, has its container images removed on October 14; TLS-compliant agents can keep sending logs through the retired HTTP Data Collector API until then.
